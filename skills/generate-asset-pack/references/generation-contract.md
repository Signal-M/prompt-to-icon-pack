# Generation contract

## All asset kinds

- Generate the exact requested count and reading order.
- Use one isolated, compact asset cluster per planned position with generous blank separation.
- Keep a uniform bright neutral exterior background.
- Do not render captions, labels, indexes, filenames, frames, separator lines, watermarks, or unrequested logos.
- Keep every subject fully visible. Keep detached decorations close enough to their subject to survive clustering.
- Reuse the same style anchor and immutable style contract across all batches.

## Icons and badges

Favor strong silhouettes, consistent stroke or material treatment, centered optical weight, and legibility at the requested delivery size.

## Emoji

Favor simple expressions, minimal props, no text, and legibility at 32–64 px.

## Stickers and avatars

Treat the reference as an identity constraint: preserve face geometry, colors, clothing, signature accessories, and body proportions. Vary expression and pose, not character identity. Generate no lettering; add captions after transparent extraction.

## Item sprites

Use one fixed camera angle, scale convention, lighting direction, ground contact, and material vocabulary. Avoid shared scenes.
